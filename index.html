Villa Villekulla!

You came to play at Pippi's house. Pippi has gone to town for sweets, and Mr Nilsson, her monkey, has locked the front door and run off with the key. Tommy and Annika are waiting outside. Get out!

Click around the kitchen, read everything, and use the things you find. Stuck? Ask for a hint, or ask the spunk. Nobody knows what a spunk is, but it's very wise.
